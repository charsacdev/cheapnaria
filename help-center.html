Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).

General

What is CheapNaria?

CheapNaria is a digital payment platform that enables users to deposit, withdraw, transfer funds, fund Deriv accounts, send money across Africa, and access other financial services securely.

How do I create an account?

Download the app or sign up on our website using your email address and phone number. Verify your email to activate your account.

Why should I verify my account?

Verification increases your transaction limits, improves account security, and unlocks additional services.

Deposits

How do I deposit money?

Tap Deposit, choose your preferred bank account, transfer the funds, and your wallet will be credited automatically once payment is confirmed.

How long does a deposit take?

Most deposits are processed instantly. In rare cases, it may take a few minutes depending on the banking network.

Can someone else fund my wallet?

No. Deposits must come from a bank account registered in your name. Third-party transfers may be rejected or reversed.

Withdrawals

How do I withdraw money?

Tap Withdraw, enter your bank details (or choose a saved beneficiary), enter the amount, and confirm the transaction.

How long do withdrawals take?

Most withdrawals are completed within a few minutes.

Transfers

Can I transfer to any Nigerian bank?

Yes. CheapNaria supports transfers to over 250 Nigerian banks and financial institutions.

Can I save beneficiaries?

Yes. You can save frequently used bank accounts for faster future transfers.

Deriv

How do I fund my Deriv account?

Select Deriv Deposit, enter your Deriv nickname, enter the amount, and complete payment.

How do I withdraw from Deriv?

Select Deriv Withdrawal, enter your Deriv nickname, specify the amount, and submit your request.

Security & Account

Is my money safe?

We use bank-grade security, encrypted transactions, and identity verification to help protect customer funds.

I forgot my password.

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page to reset your password securely.

My account is restricted.

Contact our support team for assistance. Restrictions may occur due to verification requirements or security reviews.

Fees & Transactions

Are there transaction fees?

Some services may attract processing or network fees. Any applicable fee is displayed before you confirm a transaction.

My transaction is pending.

Please allow a few minutes. If the transaction remains pending, contact support with your transaction reference.

I sent money to the wrong account.

Contact support immediately. Recovery depends on the status of the transaction.

How do I report an issue?

Tap Contact Support within the app and provide your transaction ID along with a brief description of the issue.
