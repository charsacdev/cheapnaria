About CheapNaria

Built By Traders, For Traders.

CheapNaria was established to build the payment infrastructure African traders deserve—fast, secure, and transparent. Our technology ensures money moves quickly and reliably, every time.

Why We Exist

From a Trusted Community to Financial Infrastructure.

CheapNaria was founded in 2018 to solve a simple challenge—making payments faster, safer, and more reliable for African traders. What started as a trusted payment community has evolved into a technology-driven financial platform processing transactions across Africa every day.

Today, we power Deriv funding, cross-border payments, and digital financial services with speed, transparency, and security. Our mission remains the same: delivering reliable financial infrastructure that helps people move money with confidence.

What We Stand For

Our Values.

Four principles guide every product decision we make, from how we price a rate to how fast we settle a withdrawal.

Transparency

One published rate for everyone. No haggling, no hidden spread depending on who you know.

Speed

Every payout is measured in seconds, not banking hours. We built our own liquidity pool so you never wait on a queue.

Security

Every account is manually KYC-verified by a real compliance agent, and every transaction is logged. Your data is too important to leave to an algorithm.

Reliability

24/7 automated processing means your weekend withdrawal doesn't have to wait until Monday.

How We Operate

No Middleman, Just Infrastructure.

Every trader moves through the same three-step cycle, and after the first pass, only the last step ever repeats.

Step 1

Create Account

Sign up and add your details in under two minutes. No paperwork, no branch visits.

Step 2

Verify

Upload your ID and our compliance team takes over. KYC is not automated here — a real agent manually reviews it, 24/7, because your data deserves a careful human check, not a script.

Step 3

Transact

Fund Deriv, pay suppliers, or send cross-border payments—fast, secure, and reliable.

Verification happens once, reviewed by a real person. After that, it's just Step 3, on repeat.
